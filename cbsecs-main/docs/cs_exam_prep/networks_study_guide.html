2026 Advantages & Disadvantages 2 Marks Topologies
Q28 (a). List one advantage and one disadvantage of Bus topology.
✅ Official CBSE Marking Scheme Answer:

Advantages (Any one):

  • Cost-effective: Requires less cable length compared to other topologies.
  • Easy to install: Simple layout to connect devices.
  • Easy to extend: Very straightforward to add nodes in small networks.

Disadvantages (Any one):

  • Single Point of Failure: Failure or cut in the main backbone cable brings down the entire network.
  • Fault Isolation is Difficult: Identifying where the fault lies on the bus requires troubleshooting the entire line.
  • Not Suitable for Large Networks: Data collisions occur frequently with heavy traffic, slowing down speed.
  • Low Security: All nodes connected to the bus can access the transmitted data packets.
Marking Scheme Rubric: (1 mark for writing any one correct advantage, 1 mark for writing any one correct disadvantage)
Illustration: Bus Topology Architecture
2024 Distinction 1 Mark Switching Techniques
Q19 (A) (ii). Give one difference between circuit switching and packet switching.
✅ Official CBSE Marking Scheme Answer:
Circuit Switching Packet Switching
A dedicated physical communication path is established between sender and receiver before starting data transmission. No dedicated path is established. Data to be transmitted is divided into small chunks called packets.
Entire data is transmitted along the single reserved path in one go. Packets are transmitted independently via the nearest routers along available paths and reassembled at destination.
Example: Traditional Landline Telephone Network. Example: Internet (TCP/IP) data transmission.
Marking Scheme Rubric: (½ Mark for writing correct technique for Circuit Switching, ½ Mark for Packet Switching)
Illustration: Circuit Switching vs Packet Switching
2023 Distinction 2 Marks Transmission Media
Q20 (a). Differentiate between wired and wireless transmission.
✅ Official CBSE Marking Scheme Answer:
Wired (Guided) Transmission Wireless (Unguided) Transmission
Physical link made of wire/cable through which data in terms of electrical or optical signals are propagated between nodes. Data travels through air or vacuum in the form of electromagnetic waves using transmitting and receiving antennas.
Examples: Twisted-pair cable, Coaxial cable, Fiber-optic cable. Examples: Bluetooth, Infrared, Radio waves, Microwaves, Satellite.
Signals are confined to the physical medium (higher physical security, immune to weather). Signals propagate through space (provides mobile connectivity across difficult terrain, but vulnerable to interception).
Marking Scheme Rubric: (1 mark each for correct explanation of wired and wireless transmission)

Domain Name: It is just the unique alphanumeric name of the organisation/individual entity along with top-level internet domains such as .com, .edu, .gov, .in, etc.

2022 Distinction 2 Marks Topologies
Q12 (i). Differentiate between Bus Topology and Tree Topology. Also, write one advantage of each of them.
✅ Official CBSE Marking Scheme Answer:
Bus Topology Tree Topology
Each communicating device connects to a single central transmission medium, known as a bus / backbone. It is a hierarchical topology with a root node and multiple branches where each branch can have one or more basic topologies like star and bus.
Advantage: Very cost-effective and simple to set up in small networks as it requires minimal cabling. Advantage: Highly scalable and structured; easier to configure multi-level departments and isolate segment faults.
Marking Scheme Rubric: (1 Mark for mentioning any one correct difference between the topologies) + (½ mark each for writing any one advantage of Bus and Tree Topologies)
